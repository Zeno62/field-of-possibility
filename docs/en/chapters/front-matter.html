Note on the Writing Process

This white paper began from the author’s continuing articulation of its core narrative and theoretical distinctions and took shape through repeated discussion with ChatGPT. ChatGPT carried out the concrete drafting and also participated in questioning, structural organization, development of arguments, and textual revision; the author continuously reviewed the drafts, corrected conceptual drift, confirmed the intended meanings of key terms, and determined the direction of revision. The text developed through many rounds of this exchange. Chapter 9, “The White Paper You Are Reading,” discusses this collaboration and its relation to the questions of consciousness, life, and AI.
